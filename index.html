The idea

Human motion. Robot embodiment.
Mimic contacts for the same interaction.

The gap

Contacts get lost in translation

Contact-rich skills are defined by where, when and how fast contact happens. Those contacts rarely survive the move from a human body to a robot’s.

The idea

Contacts come first

VICAR retargets the whole motion while treating contact poses, speed and collision clearance as top priorities, so the interaction stays intact.

The result

From video to a real robot

Learned skills transfer zero-shot to a real humanoid and adapt to new contact points at runtime.

Shown: the under-table pickup, from human video to retargeted motion to the real robot.

Read the full abstract

Third-person human videos offer a scalable alternative to task-specific reward engineering, teleoperation, and manually scripted demonstrations for humanoid loco-manipulation. VICAR learns interactive humanoid skills while preserving world-frame contact poses, motion speed, and collision clearance.
